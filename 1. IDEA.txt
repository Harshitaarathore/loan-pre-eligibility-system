1. Technology Stack
Frontend: Streamlit (Best for ML projects) or React.js (for a professional look).

Backend: FastAPI or Flask (to serve the ML model and rule engine).

Machine Learning: Scikit-learn, XGBoost, and SHAP (for explainability).

Database: PostgreSQL or MongoDB (to store user profiles and repayment history).

Automation: APScheduler (to run the periodic "Dynamic Update" of scores).
